Sample Itinerary

Kasol
Kheerganga

Located within the solitude of the Himalayas — green meadows, wild flora, natural hot springs, and waterfalls. A beloved 4-day trek departing every Friday from Delhi.

Day 0
Delhi → Kasol
Depart Delhi at 6 PM in a deluxe AC bus. Overnight journey through the mountains.
Day 1
A Day in Kasol
Arrive by 11 AM, check-in at Swiss cottages. Lunch, Manikaran visit, local café & market, DJ night by bonfire.
Day 2
Kheerganga Trek
Morning trek to Kheerganga. Check-in dome camps. Natural hot springs, dinner, bonfire music party.
Day 3
Sightseeing + Return
Sunrise views, breakfast, Kasol market shopping. Board bus back to Delhi in the evening.
Day 4
Back in Delhi
Arrive home in the morning — with a bag full of memories and stories to tell.
